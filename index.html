Send photos. Get files. Upload them.

Questions

The things store owners ask first.

Will this slow my product page down?

It should not. Every clip ships under 1 MB, which is smaller than a single unoptimised product photo on most stores. They are standard H.264 MP4 files sitting in Shopify's own product media, so there is no third-party player script and no app, which is where most video solutions actually cost you speed. Frame 0 opens at full brightness, so the gallery never shows a dark square while the file loads.

Do I have to ship you the product?

No. Zero physical shipping at any stage. We work from the product photography already on your store, so there is no box, no courier, no insurance and no week of waiting before anything starts.

Why does fashion cost more?

A product render animates the scene around a stationary object. A garment render has to move the garment itself through a full turn while the fit, drape and fall of the fabric stay believable frame to frame. It is a longer build with more that can go wrong, so it carries a 20% premium: $29.95 per SKU against $25.00, or $19.49 against $16.23 at catalog volume.

How fast is delivery, really?

48 hours on either 20 pack. 4 business days on either 80 pack, delivered as one ZIP named to match your SKUs. A full catalog is four times the work, so it takes longer than the small pack rather than less. The clock starts once we have your product URLs.

What does "category-tailored motion" actually mean?

For products that sit still, the background moves in a way that suits what the thing is. Rolling mist behind skincare, rising steam off coffee, sunlit orchard light behind an olive oil. For apparel it means something different: the garment itself moves, through a full model turnaround, so a shopper can see how it sits, how the hem falls and how much it moves.

Why is everything 4:5?

4:5 vertical at 1080 x 1350 px is the ratio Shopify's product media gallery is built around, and it is the shape that fills a phone screen without wasting it. A 16:9 clip loses most of the height on mobile and a square clip loses some of it. Formatting every video to 4:5 means the file drops into your gallery and sits at the same size as your existing product images, with no cropping, no letterboxing and no theme surgery.

Does it work with my theme?

Shopify supports MP4 in native product media on every modern theme. The files upload under Products > Media in your admin, the same place your images already live. Nothing to install and nothing to configure.

What if the first one is not right?

Start with the free render. Pick one product, we build the loop, and you decide from there. If it does not earn a place on the page there is nothing owed and nothing to cancel.
