Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

1. Scope

This notice explains what personal information Yeltis collects, why, and what we do with it. It covers yeltis.com and our video production services.

2. What we collect

When you request a free sample. Your name, your email address, and the product page link you submit. That is the entire form.

When you purchase. Stripe collects your payment and billing details and processes the transaction. We receive confirmation of the purchase, your name, your email and the store URL you provide at checkout. We never see or store your card number.

When you email us. Whatever you choose to put in the email.

Product photography. The images we work from, which are either supplied by you or publicly available on your store.

We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this site, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

3. Why we use it

  • To produce and deliver the video you asked for.
  • To reply to you about your order or enquiry.
  • To take payment and keep the accounting records the law requires.

If you ask for a free sample, we may follow up about it. You can tell us to stop at any time and we will.

4. Who else handles your information

We use a small number of service providers, each only for the purpose listed:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Formspree — delivering the sample request form to our inbox.
  • Vercel — hosting this website.
  • Google (Gmail) — our email.
  • AI model providers — the product images you supply are processed by third-party AI services in order to generate your video.

We do not sell your personal information, and we never have. We do not share it with anyone for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiry emails and form submissions are kept while a conversation is active and for a reasonable period afterward. Order records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Source images and delivered files are kept for the duration of our working relationship so we can re-supply files you lose, and are deleted on request.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to give you a copy of what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email ken@goyeltis.com and we will act on it. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights, including under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the UK and EU GDPR. We honour those requests regardless of where you are.

7. Cookies

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Our host may set strictly necessary cookies required to serve the page.

8. Children

This is a service sold to businesses. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

9. Changes

If this notice changes materially we will update the date above.
